# RMR Global v5.4.1 Product Owner Manual QC

## Start

1. Extract to `C:\RMR541\RMR541`.
2. Start Docker Desktop.
3. Double-click `START-PRODUCT-OWNER-TEST.bat`.
4. Wait for **Ready for Product Owner use**.
5. Open `http://localhost:8086`.

## Four-theme Golden Path

1. Sign in as RMR Owner (`dave@rmr.local`).
2. Open Kerry Laughlin Real Estate Client 360.
3. Click **Manage Branding**.
4. Confirm four preview cards appear in this order:
   - RMR Classic Blue
   - Metallic Silver
   - Metallic Gold
   - Champagne Gold
5. Select Classic Blue and click **Save & Apply**.
6. Enter Kerry's authenticated workspace and confirm the workspace uses the soft-blue design.
7. Repeat steps 3-6 for Metallic Silver, Metallic Gold, and Champagne Gold.
8. Confirm each selection changes navigation, surfaces, cards, accents, buttons, and highlights while screens and functionality remain the same.
9. Log out and back in; confirm the last selected style remains.
10. Restart the v5.4.1 app/container using the launcher sequence and confirm the style remains.

## Tenant isolation

1. Set Kerry to Champagne Gold.
2. Open CAF Client 360 and Manage Branding.
3. Confirm CAF retains its independently configured style and was not changed by Kerry.
4. Sign in as Kerry Client Administrator and confirm only Kerry can be managed.
5. Confirm cross-tenant read/write attempts are denied.

## Existing branding preservation

Confirm tenant brand name, logo, typography, three color controls, preview, Remove Logo, Reset to RMR Default, and Save & Apply remain functional.

## Existing product regression spot checks

Spot-check Client 360, CRM, opportunities, ProspectIQ boundaries, Campaigns & Social, Email & Activities, Forecasting, Reporting, Training, Team & Settings, Solutions, Website, onboarding, Client Pricing, Partner Economics, support auditing, and System Health.

## Approval

Do not approve v5.4.1 unless all four designs are visibly present and switchable, tenant isolation holds, and no accepted functionality regresses.
