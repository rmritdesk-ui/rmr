# RMR Platform v5.0 QC Findings & v5.1 Acceptance Matrix

**Baseline:** RMR Platform v5.0 Functional Production Pilot Candidate (`5.0.0-rc1`)  
**Original target:** RMR Platform v5.1 RC1 — Functional Client Experience & QC Correction Candidate (`5.1.0-rc1`)  
**RC2 status:** all application corrections remain in `5.1.0-commercial-cb1`; RC2 adds deployment reliability correction V51-QC-036 in the completed matrix.  
**Owner / approval authority:** Real Marketing Results, LLC (RMR)  
**Purpose:** Reconcile every issue reported during hands-on v5.0 installation and functional QC before changing code. This matrix is the binding correction and evidence plan for v5.1.

## Severity scale

- **BLOCKER** — prevents installation, authentication, tenant access, or the required RMR-to-client operating journey.
- **HIGH** — material workflow, security, permissions, data integrity, or usability failure.
- **MEDIUM** — important usability, clarity, navigation, or operational-quality defect.
- **LOW** — polish or convenience issue that should be corrected without expanding scope.
- **POLICY** — data/accounting policy must remain configurable until RMR makes the business decision.

## Required end-to-end acceptance journey

v5.1 cannot be declared a candidate unless the following exact sequence passes from a clean database:

1. RMR Owner completes first-run setup and logs in with the exact entered credentials.
2. RMR Owner creates a client and selects services/client prices.
3. The new client appears immediately in Portfolio, Client selector, Onboarding, Client Pricing and Client 360.
4. RMR Owner provisions/invites the primary Client Administrator.
5. Client Administrator activates the invitation and sets their own password.
6. Client Administrator logs in and sees only the authorized tenant and active/available solutions.
7. Client Administrator creates at least one CRM account/contact/opportunity/activity and updates an authorized forecast record.
8. A second tenant is created; the first client user is denied access to the second tenant by both UI and API.
9. RMR Owner enters the first client’s Client 360, views client operations read-only, and is denied any attempt to modify the client’s operating records.
10. RMR Owner returns to Portfolio without relying on browser Back.
11. Client requests an available solution; RMR receives a notification/expansion opportunity; no charge or entitlement is created until approval.
12. RMR approves/activates the service; Client Pricing, entitlement, required training and Partner Economics reflect the activation.
13. Backup is created, data is changed, rollback/restore returns the prior state.
14. Upgrade from a v5.0 fixture preserves onboarding notes, client records and service schedules.

## Partner Economics policy decisions retained for RMR review

v5.1 will implement the capability but will **not** silently decide:

- Whether partner split applies before or after allocated shared operating costs.
- Which costs are direct, shared platform, partner-specific or general RMR overhead.
- Allocation basis for hosting/infrastructure (per tenant, active user, usage, revenue, or other driver).
- Labor allocation method (time entry, standard unit cost, fixed monthly allocation, or another method).
- Treatment of sales commissions relative to partner shares.
- Whether general operating overhead is included in partner settlement or shown only in RMR management profitability.
- Accounting recognition policy for invoices, cash receipts, credits, refunds and usage authorization.

The system must preserve effective dates, source evidence and transaction traceability so RMR can choose these policies later without rewriting history.
