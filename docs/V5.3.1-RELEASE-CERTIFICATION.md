## Scope

This candidate implements only frozen Product Owner corrections PC-01 through PC-09. It preserves the accepted v5.3 product, roles, permissions, tenant isolation, Kerry and CAF data paths, CRM, ProspectIQ, websites, campaigns, one-to-one email, forecasting, reporting, training, commercial terms, Partner Economics, onboarding, service requests, upgrade and rollback behavior.

Tenant-selectable themes are explicitly excluded and preserved for v5.4 or later.

## Certified corrections

- Record-backed Portfolio and Client Success intelligence provides useful drill-down or action paths.
- Opportunity detail provides direct Mark Won and Mark Lost controls while preserving Edit Opportunity and the existing pipeline/reporting engine.
- Onboarding stages enforce objective required evidence before completion.
- Invitation state distinguishes local-link creation, delivery, acceptance and failure honestly.
- Service activation confirms commercial terms and creates an RMR operational follow-up notification/path.
- Forecast detail separates CRM-originated records from ProspectIQ-originated records without changing calculations.
- CRM Leads index presentation is corrected while preserving Open and Convert behavior.
- Solutions cards contain labels, pricing and actions without overlap at normal desktop sizing.
- System Health keeps business-language status primary and places raw technical output behind progressive disclosure.
